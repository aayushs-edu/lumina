# Home page (below the hero)

Scope: everything under the approved Aurora hero on the home route, plus square-cornering the navbar and the hero's controls. Visitor mode: Persuade (readers first, sharing second). Extension inside the hero's established world; no new identity.

Audience and job: a reader arriving from the hero should be reading a real story within one scroll, then see where inequality concentrates, then understand lumina and be invited to share.

Constraints: copy kept as written; no rounded corners anywhere on the page; no invented stats or testimonials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page below the hero is a reading room, not a dashboard. Refuses the category default of stacked rounded cards, gradient headings, glowing chips and an auto-sliding carousel with dots.

OWN-WORLD: The hero's warm light settles into flat fields: brand orange #FF6600 as a solid block, warm white #FFFCFA reading ground, deep ink #2A1A16 closing band with cream text. Baloo 2 throughout, weight and size for emphasis, never gradient text. Square corners, 1px warm rules #EADBD2, shadows only with real offset.

STORY: Today's theme names what to read; three top stories read as excerpts; the atlas shows where; the close says what lumina is and invites a story.

FIRST VIEWPORT: (below hero) Full-bleed split: left 40% orange field with "Today's Spotlight", the theme word at display size in ink, and a square ink "View Stories" button; right, "Top Stories" with three excerpt entries (quoted title, three-line excerpt, country and likes) divided by hairlines. Stacks on mobile.

FORM: Extension of the established hero world; no concept roll (surface inherits its world). Seed key: none (extension, concept-seed not run).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
